Formula Glossary

Every calculation this app makes, explained in one place. Rates and thresholds shown here are read live from the code -- if a rule ever changes, this page changes with it.

1. Per-Trade Formulas

Applied to each closed trade (a matched buy/sell or open/close pair) -- a direct port of the original spreadsheet's column logic.

Per-trade formulas: field name, formula, and explanation
Field Formula What it means
Realized Value quantity × sell_price Total proceeds from closing the position (fee-net -- see note below).
Cost Basis quantity × cost_price What the position originally cost (fee-inclusive).
Break Even strike_price + cost_price Per-unit, not multiplied by quantity. For Shares (no strike price), this collapses to just Cost Price. Informational only -- no other formula on this page depends on it.
Gain / Loss Realized Value − Cost Basis Dollar profit or loss on the closed trade.
% Gain / Loss Gain ÷ Cost Basis Return on the trade as a percentage. 0% if cost basis is 0.
Holding Period (months) (sell_date − buy_date).days ÷ 30 Approximate months held -- a simple day/30 conversion, not calendar-month-aware.
Gain / Month % Gain ÷ Holding Period (months) Return rate normalized by how long the trade was held. 0 if held under a month (rounds to 0 holding period).
Gain Type held > {{ long_term_threshold_days }} days ? "Long" : "Short" Determines which tax rate applies below.
Estimated Tax Gain × rate (gains only) Long-term rate {{ long_term_rate | pct }}, short-term rate {{ short_term_rate | pct }}. Losing trades owe $0 -- this column never nets a loss against a gain (see Section 5 for the monthly summary, which does net within a term).
Cumulative Investment running sum of Cost Basis Running total across all uploads ever made, in sell-date order -- never resets between statements.
Cumulative Gain running sum of Gain / Loss Same running-total treatment as Cumulative Investment.
Cumulative Gain % (Cum. Gain − Cum. Investment) ÷ Cum. Investment Overall return-to-date across the full trade history.

2. Tax Rules

Confirmed decisions -- flat rates, not bracket-based.

  • Long-term (held more than {{ long_term_threshold_days }} days): flat {{ long_term_rate | pct }}.
  • Short-term (held {{ long_term_threshold_days }} days or fewer): flat {{ short_term_rate | pct }}.
  • Tax only ever applies to gains at the per-trade level -- a loss never generates a negative tax / refund on its own row.

3. FIFO Lot Matching

How raw broker transactions become the closed trades that feed Section 1's formulas.

Current scope: options contracts only. Plain share Buy/Sell trades and expired-worthless contracts are intentionally excluded for now -- see below.
  • First-In-First-Out: the oldest open lot for a symbol is always closed first. No specific-lot override (a v2 feature if ever needed).
  • Options only: plain share trades are skipped entirely, regardless of action name.
  • Options multiplier: option contract quantities are converted to underlying-share-equivalent units by multiplying by {{ option_multiplier }} before matching (1 contract = {{ option_multiplier }} units).
  • Opening action: Buy to Open only.
  • Closing action: Sell to Close or Expired. An expired contract's Price/Fees/Amount are blank in the broker export -- the parser reads a blank money field as $0, so an expired contract closes automatically at $0 realized value (a total loss of the premium paid), matched against its opening lot exactly like a real sale. This is what keeps expired contracts from getting stuck showing up as still-open positions.
  • Cross-statement matching: matching runs across every upload ever made, not just one file -- a contract opened in one month's statement and closed in a much later one still matches correctly.
  • Partial fills: if a closing transaction is larger than the oldest lot, it consumes that lot fully and continues into the next-oldest lot -- can produce multiple closed-trade rows from one closing transaction.
  • Unmatched closes: if a close has no matching open lot in the uploaded history (usually because the position was opened before your earliest statement), it's flagged on the Review tab instead of silently guessed at.
  • Open positions: any lot with remaining unmatched units at the end of processing shows up as a currently-open position.

4. Fees & Cost Basis

A deliberate, more tax-accurate departure from the original spreadsheet (which ignored fees).

  • Opening-side fees are folded into cost_price: fee_per_unit = fees ÷ units, added to the lot's unit price.
  • Closing-side fees are folded into sell_price: effective_sell_price = close_price − fee_per_unit.
  • Net effect: fees reduce your gain (or increase your loss) automatically, on both legs of the trade.

5. Monthly / Period Summaries

Roll-ups shown on the Dashboard and Overview tabs, built from the per-trade rows above.

Monthly and period summary formulas
Field Formula Notes
Realized Investment sum(Realized Value) Across all trades in the period.
Yield % sum(Gain) ÷ sum(Cost Basis) Blended return across the whole period, not an average of per-trade %s.
Tax by Term (Short / Long) (gains + losses in that term) × rate Different from the per-trade Estimated Tax column -- this one nets losses against gains within the same term before applying the rate, so a losing trade can reduce the period's estimated tax. The per-trade column never does this.

6. CSV Parsing Rules

How raw broker export rows get cleaned up before anything else runs.

  • Option symbols like AMZN 08/21/2026 245.00 C are parsed into underlying ticker, expiration, strike, and right (Call/Put).
  • Adjusted contracts: a trailing digit right after the ticker with no space, e.g. AZN1 03/20/2026 90.00 C, marks an OCC-adjusted option (created when a corporate action -- merger, spinoff, special dividend, ADR ratio change, etc. -- forces the OCC to adjust existing contract terms). The digit is split off so the Ticker column always shows the real/unadjusted symbol (AZN, not AZN1) everywhere it appears (Trade Log and Needs Review both split Ticker / Expiration / Strike / Type into their own columns now, instead of one raw symbol string) -- a small i icon next to the ticker flags which rows these are (hover it for the explanation). This does not change the 100x options multiplier -- verified against a real adjusted trade where the standard multiplier reproduced the broker's reported dollar amount exactly.
  • Settlement vs. effective date: rows formatted as 08/24/2026 as of 08/21/2026 (common on Expired rows) use the as-of date -- what actually matters for holding period and tax-year purposes -- not the later settlement date.
  • Account label: pulled from the uploaded filename via a masked pattern like XXX939 (e.g. Joint_Tenant_XXX939_Transactions_...csv).

7. Income Tab (Bonus, Not Tax-Relevant)

Shown for context only -- these never feed into the gain/loss or tax formulas above.

  • Included actions: Cash Dividend, Qualified Dividend, ADR Mgmt Fee, Foreign Tax Paid, MoneyLink Transfer.
  • Totals are a simple sum of the Amount column per action type, plus a grand total across all of them.
